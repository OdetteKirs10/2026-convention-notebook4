Information for Delegates

Attendants

Assigned to assist you. Please cooperate regarding parking, crowd movement, and seat saving.

Baptism

Reserved seating on the main floor before Saturday morning's discourse begins. Bring towel and modest attire.

Voluntary Donations

Contribution boxes are located around the facility. You can also donate online anytime at donate.jw.org.

Seating Consideration

Save seats only for immediate household family and those traveling in the same vehicle.

First Aid

Reserved strictly for emergencies occurring on the convention grounds.

Volunteer Service

If you wish to assist, check in at the Information & Volunteer Service Department.
